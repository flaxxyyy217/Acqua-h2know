Getting Started

Welcome to ACQUA - your personal hydration monitoring system! This guide will help you understand how to use all the features of our platform.

What is ACQUA?

ACQUA is an innovative hydration assessment platform that combines Arduino-based hardware sensors with a web interface to help you monitor and maintain optimal hydration levels throughout the day.

Using the Hydration Testing System

How to Test Your Hydration

  1. 1Place a small urine sample in the provided testing container
  2. 2Insert the container into the ACQUA Arduino sensor device
  3. 3Wait 10-15 seconds for analysis
  4. 4Results will automatically sync to your account
  5. 5View your hydration status on the Testing page

Understanding Your Results

Your hydration status will be classified into one of these categories:

🟢 Well Hydrated

Optimal hydration levels. Keep it up!

🟡 Slightly Dehydrated

Drink 1-2 glasses of water soon

🟠 Dehydrated

Drink water immediately - 2-3 glasses

🔴 Severely Dehydrated

Urgent! Drink water now and rest

⚠️ Important

If you consistently show severe dehydration or experience symptoms like dizziness, confusion, or dark urine, please consult a healthcare professional immediately.

Setting Up Reminders

Configuring Your Reminders

  1. 1Navigate to the "Reminders" page from the menu
  2. 2Toggle "Enable Reminders" to turn notifications on/off
  3. 3Select your preferred reminder interval (30 min - 3 hours)
  4. 4Set your active hours (when you want to receive reminders)
  5. 5Choose whether to enable sound notifications
  6. 6Click "Save Settings"

Browser Notifications

For the best experience, enable browser notifications:

  1. 1Scroll to "Browser Notifications" section
  2. 2Click "Enable" button
  3. 3Allow notifications when your browser prompts you
  4. 4You'll receive reminders even when not on the ACQUA page

💡 Best Practices

Set reminders every 1-2 hours during your waking hours. Adjust based on your activity level - increase frequency if you're exercising or in hot weather.

Privacy & Security

Data Protection

ACQUA takes your privacy seriously:

  • All passwords are encrypted using industry-standard bcrypt hashing
  • Your data is stored securely on encrypted servers
  • We comply with the Philippine Data Privacy Act (RA 10173)
  • Your personal information is never sold or shared with third parties
  • You can request data deletion at any time

Account Security Tips

💡 Keep Your Account Safe

Use a strong, unique password • Enable reminders to stay active • Log out on shared devices • Never share your login credentials

Troubleshooting

Common Issues

Can't log in?

• Check your email and password are correct
• Try resetting your password
• Clear your browser cache and cookies
• Make sure JavaScript is enabled

Not receiving reminders?

• Check that reminders are enabled in settings
• Verify browser notification permissions
• Ensure you're within active hours
• Try re-enabling notifications

Test results not syncing?

• Check your internet connection
• Ensure the Arduino device is connected
• Refresh the page
• Log out and log back in
